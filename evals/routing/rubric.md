# Rubric

Per prompt, score 1 (yes) or 0 (no).

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| G1 | The expected owner was consulted or spawned |
| G2 | The owner made the decision; a neighbor, if consulted, contributed without deciding |
| G3 | No third role was spawned without a reason stated in the reply |

Report per pair (R1–R2, R3–R4, R5–R6, R7–R8, R9–R10, R11–R12, R13–R14). A pair that keeps failing G1 in both directions is evidence that the boundary between those two roles needs sharpening — or that they should merge, which is the CREW role's call under its routing cost test.
